What the plan includes

  • Aircraft: Regular and Micro cover Aerodynamics, Structures, Electrical, and their allocated engineering reserve. Two complete Micro aircraft are planned in every scenario. Existing parts and purchases are credited in procurement quantities. There is no separate Micro manufacturing allocation. A $0 Aerodynamics purchase allocation does not mean no design or analysis work.
  • Shared resources: Manufacturing, Programming, Marketing, and shared engineering reserve serve the program and count once. Management contains Travel + Competition only.
  • Travel + Competition: $16,000 = $14,405.60 base known/planned estimate + $1,594.40 contingency. Twenty travelers fly and four drive. The plan covers registrations, airfare, lodging, truck/trailer transportation, rental vehicles and core fuel; contingency allows for reasonable team meals, baggage, parking, local transport, fuel variance and other competition travel changes. These are planned costs, not bookings, commitments or completed spending.
  • Manufacturing: Approximately $2,200 includes $1,000 for new shared shelving/tools, counted once, plus fabrication, consumables and safety supplies.
  • Programming: $380 before applicable tax/shipping: $240 for one individual ChatGPT Plus subscription for 12 months, billed monthly, and $140 for two shared RGB cameras. No API credits, GPU/cloud compute, additional subscriptions or enterprise plans are included.
  • Marketing: $2,572.33 includes a $2,500 outreach/operations allowance and $72.33 field access. The $2,000 projected polos/merchandise allowance is inside the $2,500, not an additional cost. Merchandise is neither spent nor committed and receives no engineering reserve markup. Marketing owns sponsor relations, merchandise, outreach, operations and field access.
  • Engineering reserves: One $1,119.46 pool is distributed 60% to Regular ($671.68), 25% to Micro ($279.87), and 15% to Shared ($167.91): approximately $670 / $280 / $170. These are portions of the same reserve, not new expenses or additional markup.
  • Deferred purchases: Optional transmitter replacement and additional instrumentation are deferred.
