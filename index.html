2026–2027 CHALLENGE

Two aircraft.
Two classes.
One engineering program.

This season expands Cyclone Aero Design into both Regular Class and Micro Class, creating more design work, more manufacturing, more testing, and more opportunities for sponsor involvement.

REGULAR CLASS

Heavy-lift aircraft

Optimized to carry the maximum number of weighted commercially available 2-liter bottles while meeting the competition mission and aircraft constraints.

RequirementsDesignBuildTestCompete
MICRO CLASS

Compact payload aircraft

Optimized to carry heavy internal payload while overcoming externally mounted drag articles in a tightly constrained aircraft package.

CHARITABLE GIVING + TAX INFORMATION

Give through the Iowa State University Foundation.

Charitable gifts supporting Cyclone Aero Design should be processed through the Iowa State University Foundation, a 501(c)(3) public charity. Qualifying charitable contributions are generally tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law, and the Foundation records gifts and provides donor receipts.

The value of benefits, goods, or services a sponsor receives may affect the deductible portion of a contribution. Companies should consult their tax adviser regarding their specific circumstances.
