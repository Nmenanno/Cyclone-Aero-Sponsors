2026–2027 CHALLENGE

Two aircraft.
Two classes.
One engineering program.

This season expands Cyclone Aero Design into both Regular Class and Micro Class, creating more design work, more manufacturing, more testing, and more opportunities for sponsor involvement.

REGULAR CLASS

Heavy-lift aircraft

Optimized to carry the maximum number of weighted commercially available 2-liter bottles while meeting the competition mission and aircraft constraints.

RequirementsDesignBuildTestCompete
MICRO CLASS

Compact payload aircraft

Optimized to carry heavy internal payload while overcoming externally mounted drag articles in a tightly constrained aircraft package.

ONLINE DONATIONS

Give through the ISU Foundation.

  1. Visit the Iowa State University Foundation giving page.
  2. In the search bar, search “Society of Automotive Engineers”.
  3. Select “Society of Automotive Engineers International Project Fund - Engineering”.
  4. Enter the amount, gift type, and gift information, then select Next.
  5. Enter the billing information, then select Next.
  6. Enter the required payment information, then select Next.
